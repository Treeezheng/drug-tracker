## Medical scope

Drug Tracker provides personal tracking and mathematical simulations. **It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.** Curves are estimates based on limited reference data or explicitly identified saved assumptions, not measured drug levels or a personalized prediction of your response.

The app does not establish whether a dose is safe, whether medicines interact, whether a medication has cleared, or when you should take another dose. A missing curve or value means information is unavailable; it does not mean zero drug remains. Medication names, strengths and sources may be incomplete or contain errors. Check actual medication details against your prescription and package.

**Do not start, stop or change medication or dosage based on this app.** Consult your prescribing clinician. Some medications carry risks of addiction or dependence. Recording a dose or a symptom does not notify a clinician or anyone else.

## Accounts, passwords and saving

Use a strong, unique password. The hosted service uses this one password for sign-in and browser decryption through OPAQUE; you do not need a second encryption password. Keep the complete recovery key private and accessible to you. It can restore your account and records without the old password. Never send your password or recovery key by email or public message.

**Keeping your recovery key safe is your responsibility.** The app requires you to confirm that you have saved a newly issued key before continuing. That acknowledgment does not verify that a usable copy exists. A downloaded key file is unencrypted. The operator does not hold a usable copy of your key and cannot recover your password or key for you. If you lose both, the service cannot recover your encrypted records. Anyone who obtains the complete recovery key may be able to access your account and records.

Settings provides authenticated password changes, recovery-key replacement and all-device sign-out. Changing the password keeps the same recovery key. Replacing the recovery key, or recovering access with it, creates a new data key for current records and a new recovery key. These operations cannot revoke previously copied ciphertext or plaintext. The privacy policy explains the browser, device and server-metadata limits of encryption.

A guest simulation stays in page memory by default. If you choose **Remember my simulation on this device**, it is saved unencrypted in that browser. It remains separate from an account unless you explicitly choose **Sync and remove local copy** after signing in. A confirmed sync saves an encrypted simulation, not a record that medication was taken. Failed saves retain the guest copy; a copy changed by another tab or not successfully cleared can also remain, with notice in the app.

Account saves need a network connection. Unsaved changes can be lost when you close, reload or lock the app. The app is not a substitute for keeping important prescription information or a backup you can access independently. Downloaded backups, reports and recovery-key files are unencrypted; protect them appropriately.
