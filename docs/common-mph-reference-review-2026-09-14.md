Graph reconstructions use rounded landmarks and linear interpolation; values between points and beyond the last point are estimates. A mean-curve peak and the mean of individual subjects’ peak concentrations are different quantities. Scaling to another dose does not establish the individual's absorption, metabolism, response or safety.

The second independent review read the original label figures and FDA table, checked formulation/analyte/marker identities, and integrated total and partial exposure. No identity or unit mismatch was found in these five profiles. Numerical bounds are engineering checks, not clinical validation or bioequivalence criteria. Regression tests also check delayed release, separate double peaks, unchanged dose snapshots, and QuilliChew package-specific half-tablet handling.
