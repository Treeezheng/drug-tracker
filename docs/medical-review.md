# Medical and scientific review

Reviewed 13 September 2026. Scope: the supplied version 0.2 brief and the local educational tracker. This is a source/model audit, not an assessment of an individual’s prescription or safety. The app is neither a measured blood-level service nor a dose-selection system.

## Conclusion

The brief makes the right central distinctions: an administration record is not a simulation; plasma concentration is not clinical benefit; effect offset is not elimination; and missing dose history is not a medication-free baseline. Its principal label citations resolve to the stated products in the research browser. The proposed reference kernels are educational approximations with additional assumptions, not validated personal predictions.

The most important refinements are (1) do not let a published-data badge cover scaled, cross-study or extrapolated calculations; (2) preserve salt/base conventions, combination ingredient amounts and patch wear time; (3) distinguish catalog coverage from model coverage; and (4) replace old label references where a newer reviewed version is available. In particular, the 2023 Azstarys reference is outdated for current-label purposes.

## Model audit: changes that matter

1. **Published profile versus amplitude scaling.** Concerta’s label reports proportional d-MPH exposure for adult 18/36/54 mg studies, while l-MPH increases disproportionately; l-MPH is much smaller. Total-MPH proportionality is reported for 54–144 mg. Multiplying the complete 18 mg curve by any arbitrary dose is therefore still an approximation, not uniformly validated profile equivalence. Product strength availability is not model validation. Do not conflate a studied PK range with the labeled recommended dose range. [Concerta §12.3](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/021121s34s40s45lbl.pdf)

2. **IR equation check.** Independently computing `ke = ln(2)/3.5` gives `0.1980420515886 /h`. The brief’s `ka = 1.0152449556 /h` yields `ln(ka/ke)/(ka-ke) = 2.0000000000 h`; its normalized equation returns 4.3 ng/mL at two hours. This verifies the mathematics of the stated assumptions, not biological validity. A concentration interval built from `Cmax ± SD` across the whole day would be fabricated.

3. **Trace and terminal extension.** The brief’s exponential extension is continuous at its stated endpoint by construction. Under that exact formula, the 18 mg contribution is approximately 0.0020564 ng/mL at 48 h and 0.000017738 ng/mL at 72 h. These are derived illustrative values, not measured concentrations, and should not imply clinically meaningful precision. The curve’s initial 0.024 ng/mL point is explicitly a digitization artifact; never interpret it as immediate absorbed dose. Direct PDF download failed here, so the supplied SHA-256 and vector-path index were **not independently reproduced**. The official graph/table identity was checked in the research browser.

4. **Mixed MPH superposition.** Adult reference Ritalin + Concerta may be shown on a qualified common MPH scale only with the cross-study assumptions retained. This is not a combination trial and does not test cardiovascular risk, interactions, benefit or tolerability. Do not combine amphetamine, methylphenidate or dimensionless illustrations into one unqualified concentration total.

5. **Effect duration.** The brief’s 08:00 administration, 08:30 onset and four-hour-after-onset example correctly ends at 12:30. It is an assumption example. Product trial endpoints often use time since administration; adding onset again would be an error. Default effect windows should be unset or visibly generic assumptions until a product-specific or user-observed interval is supplied.

6. **History and tails.** Cross-day continuity, explicit missing prior history, pinned model versions and aggregate truncation bounds are scientifically sensible computation requirements. A finite study graph ending is not evidence of zero concentration. For a published-only mode, a necessary unsupported tail makes the total incomplete. Use “Known contributions” and propagate that state into sleep readings and exports.

7. **Patches, prodrugs and nonstimulants.** A fixed IR Bateman curve is not a scientifically justified common model for these formulations. Keep them loggable and show a separate assumption panel until a reviewed formulation-specific model exists. Do not let a user-adjustable onset parameter silently stand in for a prodrug conversion or release mechanism.

8. **No automated regimen or safety inference.** The app may compare user-entered times and display source-linked administration information. It should not compute an ideal next dose, individual insomnia probability, safe plasma threshold, mg “remaining in the body,” treatment equivalence, or concentration-based advice for exercise/studying. These require evidence absent from this brief.

## Catalog coverage delivered in `src/lib/catalog.ts`

The catalog contains 50 ingredient/formulation entries, including Ritalin IR 5 mg and Adderall IR 5 mg and their other labeled strengths; IR tablets and chewables; osmotic, bead, delayed-release and other ER forms; liquids with mg/mL and mL; patches with mg/9 h; two-ingredient Azstarys strengths; amphetamine and methylphenidate families; lisdexamfetamine; atomoxetine; guanfacine ER; clonidine ER; viloxazine ER; centanafadine; and four Metformin formulations for co-medication records (IR, ER 500/750 mg family, osmotic ER, and oral solution). Metformin is not represented as an ADHD treatment or given a product-specific concentration model. Every entry links to source metadata. Generic entries are family shortcuts and require exact package/labeler confirmation.

**Implemented model grades:** Concerta = A only for the reconstructed reference segment and conditions, with additional estimates visibly qualified; Ritalin IR = B constructed parameter approximation; all other entries = D until a model is actually established. D means this implementation has no reviewed product-specific model, not that no evidence exists. A source alone does not promote the implemented model.

Promising next A/B review candidates already have useful primary evidence: Ritalin LA, Adderall XR/Mydayis, Jornay PM, Focalin XR, Azstarys, Intuniv and Onyda XR. Their source figures/parameters must be digitized or fitted with independent checks of analyte, regimen, dose, population, food, observed domain, release and tail policy before use. No C proxy is silently assigned based solely on matching ingredients.

This is **broad coverage, not a complete all-US-products database**. Remaining work includes manufacturer/NDC-level reconciliation, generic equivalence/label validation, historical aliases (for example Adhansia XR, Ritalin SR, Metadate ER), source snapshots/checksums, market-status reconciliation, complete indication-age metadata and periodic reviewed updates. The [FDA Orange Book](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/approved-drug-products-therapeutic-equivalence-evaluations-orange-book) distinguishes approval/formulations; the [DailyMed SPL API](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/webservices-help/v2/spls_api.cfm) supports versioned label discovery. The [DEA-hosted IQVIA appendices](https://www.deadiversion.usdoj.gov/pubs/docs/IQVIA-Report-on-Stimulant-Trends-2024.pdf) are a historical discovery list, not proof of current sales. Current marketing and pharmacy stock are explicitly unverified for this local catalog.

## Verification boundaries

The current implementation limits concentration references to 18 mg Concerta and 10 mg Ritalin IR administrations without flagged alteration. Other amounts remain loggable and use a separate dimensionless illustration only after explicit assumption acceptance; source-dose scaling is not silently enabled. A second implementation review corrected liquid/unit snapshots, initial combination ingredients, four-salt Adderall records, full package-strength retention, exact decimal quantity arithmetic, fractional-solid flags and preservation of historical ingredient/labeler information during unrelated edits. Thirteen direct checks covered these cases. Patch amounts carry an explicit nominal-delivery basis; the generic D illustration does not model wear duration or absorption.

All S1–S18 medical/model/discovery URLs in the supplied brief were opened in the research browser; the updated Azstarys label and additional formulation references were located. The NICE page initially returned 403 on direct opening, but the relevant official recommendations were recovered through indexed official search. No primary source was replaced by a patient forum for a medical fact. Label presence was verified; a global latest-label/approval/marketing audit for every NDC was not completed. Numerical reconstruction checks do not constitute clinical validation.


## Final inventory review (2026-09-13)

Tablet quantity is recorded separately from package strength: 0.5 of a 10 mg tablet gives a recorded nominal 5 mg amount and deducts 0.5 tablet. This arithmetic does not establish that a particular tablet can be split. The [FDA tablet-splitting guidance](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/buying-using-medicine-safely/tablet-splitting) requires checking the exact labeling; many modified-release products must remain intact. The UI retains product-specific instructions in Details, marks fractional solid quantities as unusual, and does not apply the whole-tablet reference curve automatically. Inventory is labeled estimated; missing receipts or taken records can make it differ from physical stock.

Current references use the same scope as the chart. Earlier record-only products no longer create permanent empty panels; a retained numerical group keeps all its earlier known and unknown contributions. Adderall labeled salt totals are not amphetamine-base conversions; Azstarys components remain separate. Legacy records without ingredient snapshots are placed in an explicit unrecorded-ingredient bucket, never added to every named ingredient.
