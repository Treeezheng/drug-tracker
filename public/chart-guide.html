Reading medication charts

One medication at a time

Use Medication to display to switch between medicines, such as Adderall IR and Concerta. Changing this selection only changes the chart; it does not edit or remove your records.

The estimated total includes repeated doses of the selected formulation, including available contributions from earlier days. Different medications are shown in separate views. The app does not add them into one combined effect or estimate their interactions.

Some medicines have more than one measured component

Mixed amphetamine salts contain d-amphetamine and l-amphetamine. Their concentrations have different time courses. Use Concentration to display to view either component in the same chart. They are not added into a single reading. See the Adderall prescribing information.

What the numbers mean

mg records the administered package amount. ng/mL is the scale of the estimated reference concentration, not a measurement from your blood and not a score for how strongly a medicine works.

A * identifies an estimated reference, dose scaling, continuation, or incomplete total. Sources & methods lists the reference population, numerical parameters and assumptions for the selected medication. A smooth curve constructed from a reported peak and half-life is not a measured concentration trace.

If a formulation has no implemented concentration model, the chart explains this and shows its dose times. Missing values do not mean the medication has cleared. Brand names beneath generic names are references for recognizing the formulation; your recorded product identity is preserved.
