Reading medication charts

Group medicines by the concentration being shown

Concerta and Ritalin share a methylphenidate chart. Each dose keeps its own formulation’s curve; compatible concentrations, including available contributions from earlier days, are added into the estimated total. Use Medication to display to switch to another group, such as Adderall. Changing this selection only changes the chart; it does not edit or remove your records.

Grouping requires the same modeled compound and concentration unit, not just a similar drug name or therapeutic class. For example, a model of d-methylphenidate remains separate from a model of total methylphenidate. Combining reference curves is an estimate; the app does not model drug interactions or establish that medicines are safe to take together.

Some medicines have more than one measured component

Mixed amphetamine salts contain d-amphetamine and l-amphetamine. Their concentrations have different time courses. Use Concentration to display to view either component in the same chart. The d-amphetamine estimate can include other models of d-amphetamine, while the l-amphetamine reading includes only models of that component. The two components are not added into a single reading. See the Adderall prescribing information.

What the numbers mean

mg records the administered package amount. ng/mL is the scale of the estimated reference concentration, not a measurement from your blood and not a score for how strongly a medicine works.

A * identifies an estimated reference, dose scaling, continuation, or incomplete total. Sources & methods lists the reference population, numerical parameters and assumptions for each medication in the selected group. A smooth curve constructed from a reported peak and half-life is not a measured concentration trace.

Recording only marks medicines with no implemented concentration model. They remain available for dose records; the chart can show their dose times, but cannot supply a concentration curve. Missing values do not mean the medication has cleared. Brand names beneath generic names are references for recognizing the formulation; your recorded product identity is preserved.
